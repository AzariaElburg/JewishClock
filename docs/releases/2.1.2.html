Jewish Clock - version 2.1.2

What changed since 2.1.1. Your events, pictures and settings stay as they are.

Your request: when Shabbat and fast messages show

  • Settings → Calendar Content: new choices on the rows Candle Lighting, Havdalah, Fast Times, Parasha and Shabbat Mevarchim.
    • From midnight or From evening before (when the Jewish day begins).
    • Until its time (lighting, Havdalah, fast end) or Until midnight.
    • Parasha: All week as until now, or only From Thu evening / From Fri midnight.
    • Tomorrow: shown the evening before, the time reads "מחר 16:22".
    • Until you change them, everything shows as before.
    • To try a choice: Advanced → Debug time, then set the time back.

New

  • Deleted events go to a recycle bin: Undo right away, or later Settings → Deleted events → Restore.
  • Settings → Pictures and icons: all event pictures and the clock's icons in one place, and how many more pictures fit.
  • Replacing an icon: zoom, move and crop it, like an event picture.
  • Hebrew dates of an event: arrows for the day and the year; a year typed as 2027 offers "תשפ״ז".
  • Messages appear next to what you clicked, and stay until you click them away.

Better

  • Settings: every section open, nothing folded. New dates start at today.
  • Open lists stand out from the page; the number arrows are always visible.
  • Excel import: names each wrong row, its dates and what is wrong.
  • Reminders: up to 30 days before an event.

Fixed

  • Debug time with a Hebrew date: a short year (פ״ז) jumped to 1970.
  • The setup page's list of networks sometimes stayed empty.
  • Dates that can't be the same day (e.g. January with Nisan) are refused.
